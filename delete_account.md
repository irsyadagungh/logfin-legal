# Request Account or Data Deletion

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your **Logfin account** and all associated data, please contact us through one of the following methods:

### Method 1: Email
- **Email:** logfin.help@gmail.com
- **Subject:** "Account Deletion Request"
- **Include:** Your registered email address

### Method 2: In-App Feedback
1. Open Logfin app
2. Go to **Settings → Feedback**
3. Select type: **Suggestion**
4. Write: "I want to delete my account"

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be **permanently deleted**:

- ✅ **Account Information:** Name, email address, profile picture
- ✅ **Transaction History:** All income and expense records
- ✅ **Budgets:** All budget configurations and limits
- ✅ **Categories:** All custom categories you created
- ✅ **Settings & Preferences:** App settings, notification preferences
- ✅ **Authentication Data:** Refresh tokens, session data

---

## Data Retention in Backups

**Important:** Deleted data may temporarily remain in our **disaster recovery backups** for a limited period (typically 30-90 days) before being permanently overwritten. This is a standard practice to protect against accidental data loss and system failures.

After the backup retention period expires, your data will be **completely and permanently removed** from all our systems.

---

## Deleting Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

If you want to delete **specific data** (like individual transactions or budgets) without deleting your entire account, you can do so directly in the app:

- **Delete Transactions:** Open transaction → Tap delete icon
- **Delete Budgets:** Open budget → Tap delete icon
- **Delete Categories:** Go to Categories → Select category → Delete

These deletions are **immediate and permanent**.

---

## Processing Time

We will process your account deletion request within **30 days** of receiving it. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
